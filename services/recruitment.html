Why it matters

Recruitment & Talent Acquisition

A wrong hire is expensive. It costs salary, management time, team morale and often the goodwill of a client. Most poor hires trace back to the same causes: a vague role definition, a rushed process and decisions made on first impressions.

We slow down the parts that matter and speed up the parts that do not. You get a clear brief, a focused search, structured assessment and a hiring decision you can explain and defend.

Who it is for: Organisations hiring regularly or for a key role, and teams without in-house recruiters.

What we deliver

Everything that is included.

Role definition

We agree what the role must achieve, the skills it needs and a realistic pay range before the search begins.

Sourcing and shortlisting

Targeted outreach and screening so you meet a small number of well-matched candidates, not a large pile of applications.

Structured interviews

Consistent questions scored against agreed criteria, so candidates are compared fairly.

Reference and background checks

Offer and onboarding

Help with the offer, the contract and a first-90-days plan that gets new hires productive quickly.

Workforce planning

A practical view of the roles you will need over the next year, and when to hire them.

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Brief

    We agree the role, must-have skills, pay range and how the position fits your team and culture.

  2. Search and screen

    We source candidates, screen them against the brief and keep you informed throughout.

  3. Shortlist and offer

    We run structured interviews, check references and support you through offer and negotiation.

  4. Onboard

    We help the new hire start well, with clear expectations and support from day one.

Questions

Good to know.

Can you recruit for a single role?

Yes. You can engage us for one vacancy or for ongoing hiring support, whichever suits your business.

How do you assess candidates?

We use structured interviews against agreed criteria, supported by reference checks, so that decisions rest on evidence rather than impressions.

Do you handle onboarding after the hire?

Yes. Onboarding support is part of the service, so new hires start well and your managers know what to do in the first weeks.

What if the hire does not work out?

We agree in your proposal how replacement and follow-up are handled, so there are no surprises.

Do you recruit outside Nairobi?

Yes. We recruit for organisations across Kenya and can source candidates in the region where you operate.

How much does this cost?

We price each engagement around your team size, priorities and timeline, so there is no fixed price list. Get in touch through our contact form and we will come back to you with a clear proposal.
