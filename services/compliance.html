Why it matters

HR Compliance & Workplace Advisory

Most employment disputes do not begin with bad intent. They begin with a missing contract, an unwritten rule or a decision that was never documented. By the time a problem reaches a labour office or a court, the paperwork either protects you or it does not.

Our compliance work gives you a clear view of where you stand today and a sensible plan to close the gaps, in plain language your managers can act on. We focus on the practices that matter most to a growing business, not on jargon.

Who it is for: Employers who are growing, hiring more people, or unsure whether their contracts, records and practices would stand up to scrutiny.

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Review

    We examine your contracts, policies, records and practices, and speak with the people who run them day to day.

  2. Report

    You receive plain-language findings ranked by risk, so you know exactly what to fix first and why.

  3. Fix and support

    We help close the gaps, update your documents and brief your managers so the improvements last.

You might need this if…

  • You have grown past a handful of staff and still have no written contracts or policies
  • A disciplinary or dismissal issue has come up and you are unsure of the correct process
  • Records, payslips or leave balances are inconsistent or incomplete
  • You want to be sure statutory registrations and remittances are up to date

What you can expect

  • A clear, ranked list of compliance gaps and how to close them
  • Contracts and records you can rely on
  • Managers who handle people issues consistently
  • Fewer surprises when an employee relations matter arises

Questions

Good to know.

Will you tell us what we are doing wrong?

Yes, plainly and privately, with a ranked list of what to fix first and practical help to fix it. Our aim is to protect your business, not to criticise it.

How long does a compliance review take?

It depends on the size of your team and how organised your records are. We agree the timing in your proposal after a short discovery conversation.

Is this legal advice?

No. We give practical HR advice and implementation support. For matters that need a lawyer, such as litigation, we will tell you so and help you prepare.

Do you represent us at hearings or in court?

We can help you prepare documents and understand the process. For representation in court or formal proceedings, we will refer you to a qualified advocate.

Can we review only part of our documents?

Yes. Some clients start with contracts or disciplinary procedures and add other areas later.

How much does this cost?

We price each engagement around your team size, priorities and timeline, so there is no fixed price list. Get in touch through our contact form and we will come back to you with a clear proposal.
