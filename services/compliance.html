What we deliver

HR Compliance & Workplace Advisory

Who it is for: Employers who are growing, hiring more people, or unsure whether their contracts, records and practices would stand up to scrutiny.

  • HR compliance health check against Kenyan requirements
  • Employment contracts and letters
  • Employee relations and disciplinary guidance
  • Record-keeping and documentation standards
  • Manager guidance on handling people issues
  • Workplace advisory on request
Discuss hr compliance with us

When it helps

You might need this if…

  • You have grown past a handful of staff and still have no written contracts or policies
  • A disciplinary or dismissal issue has come up and you are unsure of the correct process
  • Records, payslips or leave balances are inconsistent or incomplete
  • You want to be sure statutory registrations and remittances are up to date

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Review

    We check your contracts, policies, records and practices against current Kenyan requirements.

  2. Report

    You get plain-language findings ranked by risk, so you know what to fix first.

  3. Fix and support

    We help close the gaps and brief your managers on handling people matters correctly.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this legal advice?

No. We give practical HR advice and implementation support. For matters that need a lawyer, such as litigation, we will tell you so.

Will you tell us what we are doing wrong?

Yes, plainly and privately, with a ranked list of what to fix first and help to fix it.

How long does a compliance review take?

It depends on your size and how organised your records are. We agree timing in the proposal after a short discovery conversation.
