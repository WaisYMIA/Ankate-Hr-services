HR Policies & Business Support

Who it is for: Organisations with no written policies, outdated ones, or documents copied from elsewhere that do not fit their business.

  • Employee handbook
  • Core HR policies and procedures
  • Employment contract templates
  • Code of conduct and disciplinary procedures
  • Leave and benefits policies
  • Policy rollout and manager briefing
Discuss hr policies with us

When it helps

You might need this if…

  • You have no employee handbook
  • Your policies were copied from elsewhere and do not fit
  • Managers make inconsistent decisions on similar issues
  • Growth or new requirements mean your documents are out of date

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Review

    We read what you have and learn how your organisation really works.

  2. Draft

    We write policies and templates in plain language that fit your business.

  3. Roll out

    We brief managers and help you introduce the policies to staff.
