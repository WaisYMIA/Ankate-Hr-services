  • Recruitment, onboarding and exits

  • Policy and compliance upkeep

When it helps

You might need this if…

  • You are growing but have no HR person
  • HR work keeps landing on the owner or the finance team
  • You want an HR partner before building a department
  • You need cover during an HR vacancy

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Scope

    We agree which HR tasks we cover and how we will work with your team.

  2. Onboard

    We take over agreed tasks and put records and processes in order.

  3. Run

    We provide regular HR support and report to leadership.

  4. Review

    We review the arrangement with you and adjust as the business grows.

Questions

Good to know.

Can we outsource only part of HR?

Yes. Many clients start with one area, such as payroll or compliance, and add more later.

Will we have one point of contact?

Yes. You will have a named contact for day-to-day HR matters.

Can we move to an in-house team later?

Yes. We can hand over systems, documents and processes when you are ready.
