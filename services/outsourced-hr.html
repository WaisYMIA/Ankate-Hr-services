Why it matters

Outsourced HR Solutions

Many businesses reach a point where HR work is too large for the owner and too small for a full-time hire. The work lands on finance or operations, and quality suffers.

Outsourced HR gives you experienced support on terms that fit: a few hours of advice, a defined set of tasks, or a full HR function. You get a named adviser who learns your business and keeps things in order as you grow.

Who it is for: Growing organisations that need HR expertise but are not ready to hire a full in-house team.

Talk to us about Outsourced HR

Hiring, onboarding and exits

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Scope

    We agree which HR tasks we cover and how we will work with your team.

  2. Onboard

    We take over the agreed tasks and put records and processes in order.

  3. Run

    We provide regular HR support and report to your leadership.

  4. Review

    We review the arrangement with you and adjust it as the business grows.

Ways to work with us

Choose the level of support that fits.

On-demand advice

Quick access to an experienced HR adviser when a people question or decision comes up. Best for small teams that mainly need a trusted sounding board.

Defined HR tasks

We take over specific work, such as payroll, recruitment, contracts or compliance upkeep, on an agreed schedule. Best when HR tasks are piling up on the owner or finance team.

A managed HR function

We run HR end to end as your outsourced department, with regular reporting to leadership. Best for growing organisations that want full coverage without a full-time team.

Questions

Good to know.

Can we outsource only part of HR?

Yes. Many clients start with one area, such as payroll or compliance, and add more over time.

Will we have one point of contact?

Yes. You will have a named contact for day-to-day HR matters, backed by the wider team.

Can we move to an in-house team later?

Yes. We can hand over systems, documents and processes when you are ready, and help you recruit your first HR hire.

How much time will you give us each month?

That depends on your needs. We agree a clear scope and level of support in your proposal, and adjust it as things change.

Do you work on site?

We combine remote support with on-site visits by agreement, so you get the right level of presence without paying for it every day.

How much does this cost?

We price each engagement around your team size, priorities and timeline, so there is no fixed price list. Get in touch through our contact form and we will come back to you with a clear proposal.
