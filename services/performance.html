Why it matters

Training & Performance Management

Talented people often underperform for ordinary reasons: unclear goals, little feedback and managers who were never taught how to manage. These are fixable problems, and fixing them is usually cheaper than replacing people.

We help you set simple, fair performance systems and build the manager skills that make them work. The aim is not more paperwork, but better conversations and results you can see.

Who it is for: Leaders whose teams are capable but inconsistent, or whose managers have been promoted without management training.

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Diagnose

    We speak with leaders and staff and review how performance is managed today.

  2. Design

    We set up simple goals, review cycles and tools that fit your organisation.

  3. Train and embed

    We train your managers to use the system confidently and follow up so it becomes routine.

You might need this if…

  • Your people are capable but output is uneven
  • Managers were promoted without management training
  • There are no clear goals or appraisals
  • Turnover is rising or engagement is low

What you can expect

  • Clear goals for every role
  • Managers who give useful feedback
  • More consistent results across teams
  • Stronger engagement and retention

Questions

Good to know.

Is this only for large teams?

No. Simple, well-run performance systems work for small teams too, and are often easier to introduce.

Do you run training on site?

Training format and location are agreed during scoping, based on your team size and needs. We deliver on site and online.

Can you train our managers?

Yes. Manager training is a core part of this service, and we can tailor it to the situations your managers actually face.

Will staff see this as extra paperwork?

We design lightweight tools and focus on conversations, so the process supports good management rather than replacing it.

How do you measure whether it worked?

We agree measures with you at the start, such as goal completion, engagement feedback and turnover, and review them together.

How much does this cost?

We price each engagement around your team size, priorities and timeline, so there is no fixed price list. Get in touch through our contact form and we will come back to you with a clear proposal.
