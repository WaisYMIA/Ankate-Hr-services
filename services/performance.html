Training & Performance Management

  • Performance management frameworks
  • Goal-setting and appraisal tools
  • Manager and leadership training
  • Employee engagement reviews
  • Retention support

Discuss training & performance with us

When it helps

You might need this if…

  • Your people are capable but output is uneven
  • Managers were promoted without management training
  • There are no clear goals or appraisals
  • Turnover is rising or engagement is low

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Diagnose

    We talk to leaders and staff and review how performance is managed today.

  2. Design

    We set up simple goals, review cycles and tools that suit your size.

  3. Train and embed

    We train managers to use the system and follow up so it sticks.

Questions

Good to know.

No. Simple, well-run performance systems work for small teams too.

Can you train our managers?

Yes. Manager training is a core part of this service.
