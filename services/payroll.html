What we deliver

Payroll & HR Administration

Who it is for: Businesses whose owners or managers are still running payroll and records themselves, or whose current process is error-prone.

  • Payroll processing and salary management
  • PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and Housing Levy support
  • Employee records and personnel files
  • Payslips and payroll reporting
  • HR reporting and documentation
  • Ongoing HR operations
Discuss payroll & hr admin with us

When it helps

You might need this if…

  • Payroll is run by the owner or a manager alongside other work
  • Deductions or payslips have contained errors
  • You are unsure you are meeting statutory deadlines
  • Employee files are scattered or incomplete

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Set-up

    We collect employee data, pay structures and statutory details, and agree the monthly calendar.

  2. Monthly run

    We calculate pay and deductions, review them with you and issue payslips and remittance schedules.

  3. Records and reporting

    We keep employee records organised and provide the payroll reports you need.

Questions

Good to know.

Which statutory deductions do you support?

We support PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Affordable Housing Levy. Rates and deadlines change, so we confirm current requirements as part of the service.

Do you make the statutory payments for us?

We prepare accurate calculations and remittance schedules. Whether we also submit payments on your behalf is agreed during set-up.

Is our payroll data kept confidential?

Yes. Payroll data is handled confidentially and shared only with people you authorise.
