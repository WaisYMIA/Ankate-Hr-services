Why it matters

Payroll & HR Administration

Payroll is the one HR task nobody forgives you for getting wrong. A late salary, a wrong deduction or a missed remittance damages trust with your team and can create penalties with the authorities.

We take the monthly workload off your desk with a repeatable process: accurate calculations, a second-person review, clear payslips and organised records. You stay in control, without carrying the detail.

Who it is for: Businesses whose owners or managers are still running payroll and records themselves, or whose current process is error-prone.

How it works

A clear process, in plain steps.

We reply to enquiries within one business day and agree scope and cost before any work starts.

  1. Set-up

    We collect employee data, pay structures and statutory details, and agree a monthly calendar with you.

  2. Monthly run

    We calculate pay and deductions, review them with you, and issue payslips and remittance schedules on time.

  3. Records and reporting

    We keep employee records organised and provide the reports you need to manage your people costs.

You might need this if…

  • Payroll is run by the owner or a manager alongside other work
  • Deductions or payslips have contained errors
  • You are unsure you are meeting statutory deadlines
  • Employee files are scattered or incomplete

What you can expect

  • Staff paid correctly and on time
  • Statutory calculations you can trust
  • Employee files that are complete and easy to find
  • Hours returned to you and your finance team

Questions

Good to know.

Which statutory deductions do you support?

We support PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Affordable Housing Levy. Rates and deadlines change, so we confirm current requirements as part of the service.

Do you make the statutory payments for us?

We prepare accurate calculations and remittance schedules. Whether we also submit payments on your behalf is agreed with you during set-up.

Is our payroll data kept confidential?

Yes. Payroll data is handled confidentially and shared only with the people you authorise.

Can you take over from our current payroll process?

Yes. We review what you have, tidy up the records and take over on a date that suits you, usually at the start of a pay period.

What do you need from us each month?

Changes to staff, pay, leave and allowances by an agreed cut-off date. We take it from there.

How much does this cost?

We price each engagement around your team size, priorities and timeline, so there is no fixed price list. Get in touch through our contact form and we will come back to you with a clear proposal.
