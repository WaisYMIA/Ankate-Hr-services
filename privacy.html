Privacy policy.

How we handle personal data submitted through this website. Last updated 9 October 2026.

Who we are

Ankate Consulting Ltd, Northpark Hub, 3rd Floor, Room TF1, Eastern Bypass, Ruiru, Kenya. Contact: info@ankateconsulting.co.ke. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form we collect your name, email, phone number, preferred contact method, organisation, team size, service of interest and your message. When you subscribe to Ankate Insights we collect your name and email address, and the time you confirmed. When you book a call we collect your name, email, phone number, topic, preferred format, the date and time, and any notes you add. When you apply for a vacancy or register your CV, we collect your name, contact details, location, experience, notice period, expected salary, cover note and CV. If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we keep that correspondence.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you proceed, provide our services (we rely on your consent and on steps taken at your request).
  • To arrange and run calls you book (steps taken at your request).
  • To assess job applications and match candidates with vacancies (we rely on your consent and on steps taken at your request).
  • To send Ankate Insights or vacancy alerts, only if you subscribed and confirmed (we rely on your consent).
  • To keep our website secure and improve it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. It may be processed by our website hosting and email providers, who handle it only to deliver those services. Job applications and CVs are shared only with the hiring organisation for the role you applied to. Form submissions are processed on our behalf by Formspree (formspree.io), and this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We may disclose it where the law requires.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for up to 12 months after the call. Job applications and CVs are kept for up to 12 months so that we can consider you for similar roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of our dealings, or as the law requires. Subscribers stay on the list until they unsubscribe.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Analytics, cookies and security

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.
