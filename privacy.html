Privacy policy.

How we handle personal data submitted through this website. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Who we are

Ankate Consulting Ltd, Northpark Hub, 3rd Floor, Room TF1, Eastern Bypass, Ruiru, Kenya. Contact: info@ankateconsulting.co.ke. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form we collect your name, email, phone number, organisation, team size, service of interest and your message. When you subscribe to Ankate Insights we collect your email address and the time you gave consent. If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we keep that correspondence.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you proceed, provide our services (we rely on your consent and on steps taken at your request).
  • To send Ankate Insights, only if you subscribed (we rely on your consent).

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. It may be processed by our website hosting and email providers, who handle it only to deliver those services. Form submissions are processed on our behalf by Formspree (formspree.io), and this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We may disclose it where the law requires.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. Subscribers stay on the list until they unsubscribe.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this page and ask for consent where required.
