Better HR operations for a cleaning and landscaping business

How a cleaning and landscaping business put clear HR policies, staff coordination systems and practical performance tools in place across its teams.

The challenge

Managing field teams day to day created HR challenges around discipline, coordination and productivity. The business needed HR systems that fitted its operational realities.

What we did

  • Put clear HR policies in place
  • Introduced better staff coordination systems
  • Provided practical performance management tools
  • Gave guidance on compliance and employee relations

The results

As reported by the client.

  • Improved discipline and productivity across teams
  • Fewer HR challenges
  • A more organised and professional work environment

Working with Ankate Consulting has greatly improved how we manage our HR operations at Rocage Cleaning and Landscaping business. They helped us put in place clear HR policies, better staff coordination systems, and practical performance management tools that have improved discipline and productivity across our teams. Their guidance on compliance and employee relations has also reduced HR challenges and created a more organized and professional work environment. The team is always approachable, responsive, and focused on delivering solutions that fit our day-to-day operational realities. We are very satisfied with their services and would confidently recommend Ankate Consulting to any business looking to strengthen their HR systems and workforce management.
