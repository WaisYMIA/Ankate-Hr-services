Common questions

Answers before you call.

Can't find yours? Ask us directly. We reply within one business day.

What does Ankate Consulting do?

Ankate Consulting is an HR consultancy in Ruiru, Kenya. We provide HR compliance and advisory, recruitment, payroll and HR administration, training and performance management, outsourced HR and HR policies for growing organisations.

Who do you work with?

We work with growing organisations in Kenya that need professional HR support but may not have a full in-house HR team. Our enquiry form covers teams from 1 to over 100 people.

Can you run payroll and handle statutory deductions?

Yes. We support payroll processing, employee records, and statutory requirements including PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Affordable Housing Levy.

Can we outsource only part of our HR?

Yes. You can outsource the whole HR function or selected tasks such as payroll, recruitment or compliance, and change the scope as your business grows.

How much does it cost?

Cost depends on your team size and the scope of support. After a short discovery conversation we agree the scope and give you a clear proposal.

Do you work outside Nairobi?

Yes. We serve organisations across all of Kenya. Advisory, payroll and recruitment support can be delivered remotely, and training or on-site support is arranged by agreement.

How quickly will we hear back?

We reply to enquiries within one business day. Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, and Saturday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.
