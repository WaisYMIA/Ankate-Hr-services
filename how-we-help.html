How we help in practice.

Three situations we see often in growing organisations, and how we approach each one.

No written policies

The situation
A growing business has no handbook and informal contracts. Managers handle disputes differently, and nobody is sure what the rules are.
What we do
We review current practice, write a handbook, core policies and contract templates, and brief managers on using them.
The result to aim for
Clear, consistent expectations and lower avoidable HR risk. See the service

Managers without management training

The situation
Strong specialists have been promoted into team lead roles. Performance is uneven and engagement is slipping.
What we do
We introduce simple goal-setting and review tools and train managers to use them.
The result to aim for
Shared expectations and more consistent team performance. See the service

HR has outgrown the owner

The situation
The owner is still running payroll, records and hiring on top of running the business.
What we do
We take on payroll, records and recruitment support, and provide HR advice on demand.
The result to aim for
A structured HR function that grows with the business. See the service
