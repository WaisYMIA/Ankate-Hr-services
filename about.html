Message from the founder

CHRP Annmary K. Mutegi

Founder and Managing Director, Ankate Consulting Ltd

Ankate Consulting Ltd was founded with a simple goal: to help businesses manage their people more effectively through practical, results-driven HR solutions that improve performance, ensure compliance, and support sustainable growth. As the Founder and Managing Director, I am a registered member of the Institute of Human Resource Management (IHRM) Kenya, which reflects our commitment to professional standards, ethical HR practices, and continuous professional development. This enables us to provide clients with reliable HR guidance aligned with current employment laws and industry best practices, allowing business leaders to focus on growth while we help build productive, compliant, and high-performing workplaces.

Where we work

Serving organisations across all of Kenya.

Our office is in Ruiru, and we support employers in every part of Kenya, from Nairobi and Mombasa to Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and smaller towns.

Advisory, payroll, recruitment support and outsourced HR can be delivered remotely. Training, workplace reviews and on-site support are arranged by agreement. Tell us where you are and we will propose the practical way to work together.
